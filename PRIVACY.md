# Privacy Policy

Prudent Ponytail is an instruction-only Codex plugin and skill. It includes no MCP server, executable code, telemetry, account system, or independent network access. Prudent Ponytail does not itself collect, transmit, or store personal data.

Codex and any tools used while following the skill may process data under their own privacy policies and user-approved settings. Prudent Ponytail does not change those policies or permissions.

Privacy questions can be raised through the [Prudent Ponytail issue tracker](https://github.com/mssoftjp/prudent-ponytail/issues).
